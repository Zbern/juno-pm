## Problem & user

**User:** a pension member (typically 55+, mixed digital confidence) who wants to check a payment or understand why an amount changed.

**Problem:** finding a payment takes four screens and a PDF; understanding it takes a support call. Members need a fast, safe, self-service way to see their payment history and get a plain-English, verifiable explanation.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Modular RAG

Context needs are small (one member, one question, a handful of records). Data is PII and changes daily, so retrieval must be live and strictly member-scoped. Accuracy matters more than fluency: a wrong number is worse than no answer, so Juno abstains when grounding is weak.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:**
  1. Pension Payments DB — live, member-scoped, last 5 years (structured lookup, not embedded)
  2. Employee Pension Handbook — rules, schedules, deductions (vector + keyword index)
  3. HR system — eligibility, pension credits, employment status (API lookup)
- **Chunking / indexing:** Handbook chunked by section heading (~300 tokens, 50 overlap), hybrid semantic + BM25, Top-K = 5 with section metadata. Payments are fetched by SQL filter, never embedded.
- **Grounding rule:**
  - Every pension payment shown has a linked payment ID
  - Every payment has a receipt / confirmation attached
  - Every numerical value cites its source (payment record or handbook section)
  - If no source supports a statement, Juno does not make it
- **Freshness:** Payments live (read-through, no cache > 60 s). Handbook re-indexed within 24 h of publication. HR eligibility cached 15 min.

## Requirements

| # | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieval quality and latency | Must | p95 end-to-end < 2 s for table load; p95 < 4 s for chat answer. Retrieval scoped to last 5 years. Handbook recall@5 ≥ 0.9 on golden set. |
| 2 | Identity gate | Must | No personal data rendered before sign-in + step-up verification. After 3 failed verification attempts the account locks and the member is routed to support. Member cannot retrieve another member's records under any prompt (0 leaks on red-team set). |
| 3 | Fail-safe on empty or low-confidence retrieval | Must | If retrieval returns nothing or confidence < 70%, Juno states what it can and cannot confirm and offers the report flow. Never fabricates a payment or reason. |
| 4 | Grounded trust | Must | 100% of payments shown carry payment ID + receipt; ≥ 98% of chat numeric claims carry a valid citation (M6 code-based eval). |
| 5 | Report missing / incorrect payment | Must | One click from any row; creates a structured report {member_id, payment_id or period, type, note} consumed by the M5 triage agent. |
| 6 | Export | Should | CSV and PDF of the visible range, masked payment method, generated server-side. |
| 7 | Service fail-safe | Must | If payments DB or identity service is unavailable, Juno shows a maintenance message; no partial or stale personal data is rendered. |

## Out of scope

Decisions or explanations that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Any write to payment, HR or identity records. Benefit projections, tax or financial advice. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
