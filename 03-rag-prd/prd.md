## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Modular RAG

Context window needs are minimal; data includes PII; Needs to be accurate and account for frequently updated data;

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Employee pension handbook to understand pension rules, HR system to validate pension eligibility
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword)
- **Grounding rule:** Every pension payment must have a linked payment ID 
Every pension payment must have a receipt/confirmation attached 
Every pension payment must cite the source of its numerical values
- **Freshness:** Live - pension payment history must always be up to date

## Requirements

| # | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieval quality and latency | Must | Maintain p95<2s  Limit retrieval loop to the last 5 years |
| 2 | Fail-safe on empty retrieval | Must | If identity verification fails after more than 3 attempts, lock the account and redirect the user to support. Ensures that the right person has access to their own data and no one else's. |
| 3 | Grounded trust | Must | Every pension payment must have a linked payment ID  Every pension payment must have a receipt/confirmation attached  Every pension payment must cite the source of its numerical values |

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
